Privacy Policy for Jumptoon

Last Updated: August 3, 2026

Thank you for playing Jumptoon. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and shared when you play the game.

Information We Collect

Jumptoon does not require users to create an account and does not intentionally collect personally identifiable information directly from players.

However, the third-party services used by the game may automatically collect certain information necessary to provide their functionality, including:

  • Device information (such as device model and operating system)
  • IP address
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Diagnostic and crash information
  • Gameplay and usage information

Advertising

Jumptoon uses Unity Ads to display advertisements.

Unity Ads may collect information such as device identifiers and usage data to provide, personalize (where applicable), measure advertisements, and help prevent fraud.

For more information, please visit:
https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

Online Leaderboards

Jumptoon uses Unity Leaderboards to allow players to submit and view high scores.

When using online leaderboards, Unity may process information necessary to provide the service, including:

  • Your chosen display name
  • Your leaderboard score
  • Anonymous player identifiers
  • Technical information required to operate the service

For more information, please visit:
https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

User-Generated Content

Jumptoon allows players to choose a display name that appears on online leaderboards.

To help maintain a respectful environment, display names are automatically checked using profanity filtering before they are accepted. Usernames containing offensive or inappropriate language may be rejected.

The developer reserves the right to remove or moderate inappropriate leaderboard content if necessary.

Data Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect information processed through the game and the third-party services it uses. However, no method of electronic transmission or electronic storage is completely secure, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

Data Deletion Requests

If you would like your data associated with Jumptoon to be deleted, you may submit a data deletion request by contacting the developer via email.

Email: aldoxhako@outlook.com
Subject: Jumptoon Data Deletion Request

To help us identify your data, please include:

  • Your leaderboard display name.
  • The approximate date you submitted your score.
  • Any additional information that may help identify your leaderboard entry.

Once your request has been verified, your leaderboard data and any associated information under our control will be deleted within a reasonable timeframe, unless we are required to retain certain information for legal or security purposes.

Children's Privacy

Jumptoon is intended for a general audience and is not directed specifically toward children under the age required by applicable law.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
